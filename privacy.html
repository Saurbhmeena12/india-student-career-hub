Privacy

Privacy policy

Short version: we collect the minimum needed to match you with opportunities, we do not sell it, and you can delete it at any time.

Last updated 31 October 2026

Who we are

India Student & Career Hub is an independent information platform. It is not a government website and is not affiliated with any ministry or department. We link to official portals; we never act on their behalf.

What we collect

DataWhyRequired?

What we never ask for

We will never ask for your Aadhaar number, OTP, bank details, income certificate number or login credentials for any government portal. The assistant actively blocks questions containing Aadhaar-style numbers, OTPs and passwords. If anyone claiming to be us asks for these, it is a scam — report it through the contact page.

Cookies and local storage

  • Session cookie — keeps you signed in. HttpOnly, cleared when you sign out.
  • Theme and language preference — stored in your browser, never sent to us.
  • Top-bar dismissal — remembered for 7 days in your browser.
  • We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or third-party analytics.

Artificial intelligence

The assistant answers from our own verified records. If a Gemini API key is configured, your question is sent to Google's free-tier API to phrase the answer; that free tier may be used to improve Google's models, so never type personal or sensitive information into the assistant. If no key is configured, questions are answered entirely on our server and never leave it.

How long we keep it

  • Account data — until you ask us to delete it.
  • Newsletter email — until you unsubscribe.
  • Link-verification logs — 90 days.
  • Contact messages — 12 months.

Your rights

You can ask us to export or delete everything we hold about you, correct any detail, or stop all email. Write from the address on your account through the contact page and we will act within 30 days. Deletion is permanent and removes your saved items and tracker history.

Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Sessions use signed tokens. Sign-in endpoints are rate-limited. We keep the platform patched and run automated link checks on the official portals we reference. No system is perfect — if you find a vulnerability, please tell us privately before publishing it.

Children

The platform is used by school students. We ask for no more data than the table above, and a parent or guardian may request deletion of a minor's account at any time.

This is a plain-language policy, not legal advice. Before a public launch in a commercial capacity, have it reviewed against the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 by a qualified lawyer.
