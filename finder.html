Nothing is stored or sent anywhere except this server. We never ask for Aadhaar, OTPs or income certificates.

How matching works

  • Qualification — we read the requirement text. If it says “Graduation in any discipline”, a graduate matches, a 12th-pass student does not.
  • State — “All India” always matches. A Rajasthan scheme only appears to Rajasthan students.
  • Category & income — a reserved scholarship hides when your category or income does not fit.
  • Nothing hidden silently — if the notification does not say, the record is still shown and labelled check notification.

This is a preliminary filter, not a guarantee of eligibility. The official notification always decides.
